Pluginarium

Your plugins as instruments in the studio. Pluginarium makes the Mac one more instrument in a dawless setup: start it, load a configuration and play, without opening a DAW.

Download for macOS Version 0.1.0 for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Read the manual.

What it does

Load and play

The app opens on your saved configurations. Pick one and its plugins load with their sounds and routing. A configuration always opens: a missing plugin or device shows on its own channel, and the other channels play.

A stack of channels

A configuration is a row of channels. Each one holds an instrument plugin or a standalone effect, with its own MIDI input, audio output and effects. Effects run in series, or up to three side by side in parallel.

Routing that follows the plugin

A channel shows only the inputs its plugin can use: MIDI in, Audio in, Sidechain in. New channels take their routing from position tables you set once, so one plugin on your usual outputs takes a few letters and Return.

Part of the studio

A standalone effect takes a hardware synth from an audio input and sends it to an audio output. Plugins with an audio or sidechain input read the inputs of your audio interface.

Follows the MIDI clock

Plugins get the tempo from the MIDI clock source you pick. A Follow Start/Stop switch on each plugin decides whether its internal sequencer also starts and stops with that clock.

Named presets

Save a plugin's sound under a name and use it in every configuration. Plugins that list factory presets as Audio Units show them next to yours.

What it needs

  • macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon.
  • Audio Units. VST and VST3 plugins are not hosted.
  • An audio device with outputs. With more outputs, each plugin can have its own channels on your mixer.

What it leaves out

Pluginarium is a host, not a DAW. It has no faders or panning, no recording, sequencing or arranging, and no MIDI learn yet. Levels are set on your mixer and in the plugins. The manual lists the limits of this version.

Download

The disk image is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings. Drag Pluginarium to Applications. Plugins are not included; the app hosts the Audio Units already on your Mac.

Build from source

You need the Xcode command line tools with Swift 6; no Xcode project is involved.

git clone https://github.com/jiripisa/pluginarium.git
cd pluginarium
./run.sh
